# Decision Rules & Assumptions

| Rule / Parameter | Value | Reason | Source / Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Salaried FOIR Cap** | 50% | High income stability allows higher debt service capacity. | Standard Indian Bank Policy Baseline[cite: 1] |
| **Self-Employed FOIR Cap** | 40% | Adjusted for revenue volatility and tax deductions. | Lender Credit Risk Framework[cite: 1] |
| **Informal FOIR Cap** | 30% | High cash flow variability requires larger safety buffers. | NBFC / MFI Micro-lending Guidelines[cite: 1] |
| **No Credit Score** | Neutral (650–700) | Missing score broadens ranges without auto-rejecting. | System Default Strategy[cite: 1] |
| **Hard Red Flag Stop** | >0 Bounces OR >55% FOIR | Prevents predatory loan trap progression. | Responsible Lending Standards[cite: 1] |
| **LAP LTV Ceiling** | 50% Property Value | Mortgaging unencumbered assets secures lower rates. | Housing Finance Guidelines[cite: 1] |
| **All-in APR Markup** | Base Rate + 1.2% - 2.5% | Accounts for 1–2% processing fees + GST. | RBI APR Disclosure Mandate[cite: 1] |
